PULSE.

Know what you can spend, without doing the math.

PULSE is a free money app for Gen Z Bharat. It works out what you can safely spend until payday after bills, SIPs and the money you're saving, so you never have to do the maths yourself.

  • Safe-to-spend until payday, updated every time money moves
  • Life plans like a Goa trip or a new laptop, with ahead or behind tracking
  • Split bills with friends and settle up in the same app
  • SIPs deducted automatically each month
