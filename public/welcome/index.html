Your money, your rules

We never ask for your bank login.

  • No bank login, card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Ever.
  • Never reads your SMS, contacts, photos or location.
  • No ads. No ad trackers. No loan offers. We don't sell your data.
  • You type what you spend, so PULSE only knows what you tell it.
  • Copy a backup or delete your account any time, from inside the app.

The honest bitWhen you sign in, your data is saved encrypted on our servers so it's on every phone you use. We hold the key, so we could open it. We only do that to run PULSE, fix a problem you report, or if the law asks.

Questions

Still not sure? How PULSE works has the full story.

What does Safe to spend mean?

The money that's actually free until your next payday, after bills, SIPs, what you're saving this cycle and a small safety buffer. You see it as a total and as a daily amount.

Is PULSE free?

Yes. No ads, no loan offers.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. PULSE never asks for bank logins, card numbers or UPI PINs. You type your balances and expenses yourself.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on your own device. Once you sign in, it's also saved encrypted on PULSE's servers, so it's there on every device you use. It's not sold and there are no ads. Privacy Policy

Can PULSE track my SIPs, FDs and RDs?

Yes. Add each one with its amount and date. PULSE records it every month and keeps it aside before payday.

Can I split bills with friends?

Yes. Split equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares, with groups and settle-ups.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open PULSE in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline too.

What if my salary comes on a different date?

Safe to spend runs from your payday to your next payday, whatever date that is.
