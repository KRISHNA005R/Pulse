How safe to spend is calculated

An example for someone paid on the 4th, checking on the 27th.

Available now (bank + cash)₹8,420
Bills due before payday−₹2,100
Plans you're saving for this cycle−₹2,500
Safety buffer for surprises−₹980
Safe to spend₹2,840

That's about ₹405 a day for the 7 days until payday. It updates every time you log money in or out.

Android & desktop

  1. Open PULSE in Chrome or Edge.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open PULSE in Safari.

Questions

What does safe to spend mean?

It's the money that's actually free until your next payday. PULSE starts with what's in your bank and cash, then keeps aside bills due before payday, SIPs due before payday, what you planned to save this cycle, and a small safety buffer. What's left is safe to spend, shown as a total and a daily amount.

Is PULSE free?

Yes. PULSE is free to use. There are no ads and no loan offers.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. PULSE never asks for bank logins, card numbers or UPI PINs. You type your balances and expenses yourself, or scan a receipt.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone or laptop, inside your browser. Nobody else, including us, can see it. You can copy a backup code to move it to another device, and erase everything from Settings at any time.

Can PULSE track my SIPs?

Yes. Add a SIP, RD, PPF or NPS with its amount and debit date. PULSE records it automatically every month, keeps it aside before payday and shows what it could grow to.

Can I split bills with friends?

Yes. Split equally, by exact amounts, percentages or shares, create groups like a trip or a flat, and record settle-ups. Your share also counts toward your plans and safe-to-spend.

Can I install PULSE like an app?

Yes. On Android or desktop Chrome, tap Install. On iPhone, open PULSE in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and works offline.
