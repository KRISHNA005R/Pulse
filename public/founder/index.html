What Krishna built

PULSE is a money app made for salaried Gen Z in Bharat, designed and developed at Krsna Studios in Mumbai. It works in any browser, or installed like an app on iPhone and Android.

  • Safe to spend: what is actually free until payday, and how much that is per day.
  • Can I afford this? See what a purchase does to your month before you buy.
  • Life plans: save for a trip or a laptop and see if you are ahead or behind.
  • Split with friends, track SIPs, subscriptions and bills.
  • Private by design: no bank login, no ads, and your data is never sold.
